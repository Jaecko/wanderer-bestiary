# Wanderer Bestiary: credits

Wanderer Bestiary is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (see LICENSE).

Its creature data (Data.lua, Habitat.lua and Names_*.lua) is generated from:

- The CMaNGOS classic database, https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db (GNU GPL v3): the creatures, their levels,
  rank, kind and family, health, mana, blows, armour, models, spells and gold, and their spawn places (habitat).
- pfQuest by Shagu, https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest (MIT licence, below): where the creatures stand, their side,
  and their names in nine languages.
- The tables of the WoW Forever game client: the zones, and the game's own statistics about a creature
  (a boss's kills, the deaths from a creature).
