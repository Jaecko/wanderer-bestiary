# Wanderer Bestiary

A bestiary of the creatures of the old world you may fight, dungeons and raids included, and your hunting log. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4.49 or later.

- Every creature with its 3D model (drag to turn it, the wheel to bring it closer), its levels, rank and kind, where it lives (a click: the way there), its health, blows, armor and spells, the gold it carries.
- A creature you never met stays in shadow; met, its model and name appear; killed once, its strength and spells. "Keep the surprise" hides the level and home of a boss, rare or elite you never met.
- Your hunting log for each character: kills, the first and last time, what you looted, the creatures that killed you, your series without dying. What the game already counted is taken in from the start.
- Records: creatures discovered and killed, your most hunted, your nemesis, rares, elites and bosses, your most complete zone. "Here" shows the creatures of the zone you are in.
- A small notice for a first elite, rare or boss, and quiet milestones. A line under a creature's name: how many you killed, or never met.
- Kills counted carefully without the combat log, never twice, never a creature killed by another.
- With [Wanderer Quests](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-quests): a creature on a quest's sheet opens its card beside it. With [Wanderer Maps](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-maps): its habitat drawn on the atlas, the creatures your quests ask for listed there.
- Coming from KillTrack: your counts brought in, shown first, never lowered, and the import can be taken back.

Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-bestiary). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
