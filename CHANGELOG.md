# Wanderer Bestiary 1.0.3

### Fixed
- A kill at skull level now counts for the highest level brought down.
- Records: "Most complete zone" stays with the counts by kind, never under the import heading.
- "Keep the surprise" also hides the way to a boss, rare or elite you never met.
- The Here list scrolls and searches smoothly, even with the bestiary open while you hunt.

### Changed
- Places on the card follow the family's clicks (Shift a map link, Ctrl a step of the route).
- The card closes with Escape and can be moved when it stands alone.
- Loot squares link with Shift; a search with no result says so.
- A first kill notice shows a hunter's mark, or a skull for a boss.
