# Wanderer Bestiary 1.0.0

First public version.

### New
- A bestiary of the creatures of the world you may fight, dungeons and raids included (3,647 of them): their 3D model (dragged to turn it, the wheel to bring it closer), their levels, rank and kind, where they live (a click: the way there), their health, blows, armor and spells, the gold they carry.
- A creature you never met stays in shadow ("???"); met, its model and name appear; killed once, its strength and spells.
- Your hunting log for each character: kills, the first and last time, what you looted from each creature, the ones that killed you, your series without dying.
- What the game already counted is taken in from the start: your kills and deaths in all, the kind you killed the most, every dungeon and raid boss you killed (as many times as the game says), the deaths from some creatures.
- The list shows round portraits ringed in the colour of their rank (gold elite, blue rare, red boss), your kills in gold, and how much of the world you discovered on a golden line.
- Records: creatures discovered and killed, your most hunted, your nemesis, the highest level brought down, your first kill, rares, elites and bosses, your most complete zone.
- "Here" shows the creatures of the zone you are in, "All" every one, with a search and a kind.
- A small notice for a first elite, rare or boss (a click opens its card), and quiet milestones (a creature killed 25, 100, 500 or 1000 times, your kills in all).
- Kills counted carefully without the combat log: your target or a creature fighting you or your pet, seen dead and not another's; in dungeons the game's experience words and the end of a boss encounter; a corpse looted; never twice for the same kill, never a creature killed by another, a death by a player never blamed on a creature. What you loot stays light (12 items per creature).
- "Here" shows how much of the zone you met and killed; Records count each kind.
- "Keep the surprise": a boss, a rare or an elite you never met hides its level and where it lives.
- A line under a creature's name (Wanderer's label, the game's tooltip): killed ×12, or never met. Never in dungeons.
- Its habitat, point by point: "On the atlas" on its card draws where it lives on Wanderer Maps' atlas (the surprise kept for the bosses and rares you never met).
- Coming from KillTrack: Records offers to bring your counts (while KillTrack is loaded too), shows what would change first, never lowers a count, and the import can be taken back.
- With Wanderer Quests: a creature on a quest's sheet opens its card beside it. With Wanderer Maps: the creatures your quests ask for on a map are listed in the atlas, a click opens their card.
- Works with the game's controller interface: the cross moves through the lists, the sticks turn the model and bring it closer.
- Needs Wanderer 1.4.49 or later.
